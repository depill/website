Welcome to depill. I’m David Fannar Gunnarsson. I work for Globus Group and am based in Iceland. Supergut ehf is where I work on my side missions, and it operates this site.

This is my personal space for side projects and things I’m exploring: AI development, process mapping, and the practical work of making enterprise systems useful.

## What belongs here

Short notes when an idea is still taking shape. Longer articles when a subject needs more room. Documentation when the details matter enough to keep them somewhere useful.

The common thread is the relationship between software and the work around it—in telco, manufacturing, wholesale, and other enterprise environments. I often find that useful process knowledge is locked behind a wall. I want to share more of it openly, with examples and documentation people can actually use.

## Tools to share
